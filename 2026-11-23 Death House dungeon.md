**Party:** Eldric Lightbearer, Melody, Vander Nahe, and Gourmet the flying lemur.

**Where we started:** deep in the dungeon below the Durst house, at about 9 in the evening. Midnight was three hours away. Nobody had a spell slot left. A muffled chant came through the walls from somewhere below.

## The ghoul in the corridor

The party searched the cultists' sleeping quarters and found a few coins in the moldy straw.

The next corridor smelled of death. Old bones lay along the floor. A hand pushed out of the dirt, and a ghoul pulled itself free.

- The ghoul swung at Vander, missed badly, and fell on its face.
- It kept talking during the fight: "We are perfect. We are immortal." and "Help us live forever."
- Eldric squeezed past Melody in the narrow tunnel to reach it.
- Gourmet distracted it, and Vander drove his sword through its ribs.
- Eldric cut off its head with the smallest hit of the night.

## The shrine

At the intersection the chant was louder to the north. The party went the other way.

They found a room with skeletons shackled to the walls. In an alcove stood a painted wooden statue: a gaunt, pale man in a black cloak, one hand on the head of a wolf, the other hand holding a smoky grey orb. Five ashen shadows were burnt into the walls.

- The party recognised the man as **Strahd**, the name on the letter they read upstairs. He was a count long ago. He and many of his people vanished without a trace.
- Near the orb, voices spoke inside their heads: "His gaze burns upon us. The Dark Lord's eyes are always watching."
- Vander's shadow began to twist and fray. It got worse each time he stepped closer to the statue.
- Eldric sprinkled holy water on the floor. Nothing happened.
- Eldric and Melody told Vander to leave the statue alone. They told him several times.

### Gourmet and the amber shard

The party remembered the note: present the shard and speak the name of the beast. The shard was still in the hidden door upstairs, so Melody sent Gourmet to get it.

Gourmet got the shard out. The door then closed with Gourmet on the wrong side. Gourmet put the shard back, and then tried to hold the door open with a chewed stone. The door stuck.

### Vander and the cloak

Vander climbed onto the platform and pulled the cloak off the statue.

It felt as though a dark, ancient evil had turned its eye on the party. The shadows on the walls came for them.

## After the shadows

The party survived the shadows and put the cloak back on the statue. They got the amber shard back. They also found a secret tunnel that leads up and out into the woods, and a stash of treasure.

### Loot

- A folded cloak of deep maroon velvet with a red lining and a hood. Vander is wearing it. It needs more time to understand.
- Potions of healing, shared among the party
- A flask of alchemist's fire
- A chain shirt, carried in a backpack
- A bull's-eye lantern
- Thieves' tools
- A mess kit

## The way down

A stair went down into a cold room with mist on the floor. Here the words of the chant were clear for the first time:

> He is the Ancient. He is the Land.

The alcoves in the walls held grim trinkets. Among them were a clawed yellow hand on a rope, a knife of human bone, an orb made from a nothic's eye, a cloak of stitched ghoul skin, a hag's finger, a shrunken halfling head, and a dire wolf's tongue in a box.

A rusted portcullis blocked the passage to the chanting. The party took the long way round, through an empty prison of cells and shackles.

## The ritual chamber

The chamber was flooded to the knee. In the middle, an eight-sided dais rose out of the water with a blood-stained altar on top. Chains hung above it. The chanting stopped when the party walked in.

- A white bundle the size of a baby lay on the altar, with fleshy tendrils around it. The tendrils had human teeth.
- The tendrils ran to a hole in the far wall. Something large was breathing slowly in the dark behind it.
- Gourmet flew over. Two words were carved into the top of the altar: **Feed him.**
- Gourmet unwrapped the bundle. Inside was a rusted, serrated dagger, stained with old blood.

When one of the party stepped onto the dais, thirteen dark figures in black robes appeared on the ledges. They held torches that burnt with black fire.

> One must die.

The cult wanted a sacrifice. The party talked about it and chose to fight. As soon as the dagger left the dais, the chant changed:

> Lorghoth the Decayer, we awaken thee.

The figures vanished. The mound of flesh and bone in the cave woke up.

## Lorghoth the Decayer

- Melody held up the amber shard and shouted "Walter!" The mound did not obey.
- Melody insulted it. She continued to insult it for the full fight, and the insults hurt it badly.
- Eldric raised his holy symbol and turned it. The mound fled into a corner and shook there in fear. This gave the party time to plan.
- The party tried the shard several more times. The mound noticed them, and it stayed too frightened to obey.
- The party offered a sacrifice. Gourmet lay down on the altar, and Eldric used the ritual dagger. Gourmet vanished in a puff. His last words were unkind. The cult's hunger was not satisfied.
- The next insult broke the fear, and the mound rolled straight at the party.
- It slammed, shook the ground until people fell in the water, shot splinters of bone, and burst a pustule of rot over the front line.
- Vander poured his holy water over his sword. His next hit, with a divine smite, was the biggest of the campaign so far. One more hit tore the mound open.

## Walter

Inside the mound was a rib cage, and inside the rib cage floated the body of baby Walter, wrapped in tentacles. The creature rose again at full strength.

- It bit, grabbed with tentacles, and sprayed blood and gore that blinded Vander twice.
- Vander dropped to a single hit point. Eldric's healing kit and the potions kept everyone on their feet.
- The rib cage opened each time the creature sprayed. Walter was exposed for a moment each time.
- Melody's shocking grasp stopped the creature from pulling Walter back inside.
- The creature grew faster and angrier. Melody presented the shard and ordered it to calm down, and this time it bucked and flailed as it had never done before.
- Eldric called down a sacred flame on Walter. The baby burst into fire and screamed. The whole creature fell into ash.

Then the house began to shake.

## Where we stopped

- The beast is dead. The party is still in the ritual chamber, and the house is shaking around them.
- It is close to midnight.
- Everyone is hurt, and nobody has a spell slot.
- Gourmet is gone until Melody can summon him again.
- Vander is wearing the maroon cloak.
- Experience: 500 of 900 towards level 3 at the start of the night. The reward for this session comes next time.

## Table rules we settled

- Fights use minis on the table. The map screen is for exploration.
- A critical hit rolls double dice.
- Every second diagonal step costs 10 feet.
- Flanking is not used.
- A potion is a bonus action, to drink or to give to someone else.
- Oil on a weapon takes an action.
- Vicious Mockery is a saving throw, so it works normally next to an enemy.
